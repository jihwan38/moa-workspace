# Three-term scope correction

Keep the frozen E137 four-term representation `[1,16,2^22,2^27]` and its ninth boundary case. This CPU-only follow-up created no three-term kernel, build or NPU measurement, and authorizes no term-count tuning.

The proposed normalized error bound `2^-32` for three terms `[1,16,2^22]` is false. Reusing the frozen E136 scalar model on its 19,328 nonnegative normalized BF16-significand/F32 values (negatives checked by symmetry) gives maximum error **`2^-19`**, with cast-input maxima `[255,128,256]`. The error comes from ordinary E4 rounding of the amplified third residual, not only final subnormal underflow.

One exact witness is `u=0.0000324249267578125`. Its first cast is zero; its second cast input `16u=0.000518798828125` also rounds to zero. The third cast input is `2^22 u=136`, which rounds to `128`; restoration yields `0.000030517578125`, losing `2^-19`. With `s=2^-7` and an amax anchor of one, the original BF16 input is `x=2.5331974029541016e-7`, within `[-1,1]`. This invalidates downstream projection/RMS estimates that assumed the smaller normalized bound. It does not by itself establish a complete-kernel tolerance failure.

The fixed public O generator has a narrower pointwise support: `BF16(-1+2k/2^24)`. The same three-term scalar calculation has zero mismatches across 2,177 nonnegative support values and 21,887 admissible value/scale pairs over 24 amax binades. **That discrete-grid result must not be used as a general precision guarantee for BF16 inputs bounded by `[-1,1]`, or as a justification to rely on a fixed PRNG's support.** The witness above is outside that grid. QKV post-RMS values have no inherited O-grid guarantee either.

The ninth E137 validation input deliberately retains a related distinction: with global `s=2^-7`, `x=129*2^-29` has normalized `u=129*2^-22`. Its third E4 term rounds `129` to `128`; the fourth term carries the remaining unit as `32/2^27`. This exercises fourth-term restoration and shared global scaling across K shards. The actual oracle owns its channel-scale choices and independently checks its F64 reference; this note does not supersede its result.

Neither scalar reconstruction nor discrete-grid exactness establishes native dot accuracy, F32 summation order, the first BF16 rounding boundary, RMS behavior or whole-O tolerance. The associated [CPU result](three-term-scope-result.json) binds the unchanged model source and preserves the finite domain, witness and limitations. Reproduction calls `analyze(domain(), [1.,16.,2.**22])` and `generator_grid_check([1.,16.,2.**22])` from [the frozen E136 model](../../qkv-native-weight-resident/numerical/moa-e4-fourterm-audit.py); their results were compared exactly with the original temporary audit before this note was frozen.
