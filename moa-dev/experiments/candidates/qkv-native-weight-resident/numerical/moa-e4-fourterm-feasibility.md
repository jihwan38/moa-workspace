## Numerical result

Scale is `s=2^(floor(log2(max(amax,2^-119)))-7)`, normalized `u=F32(x/s)`; zero/tiny scale floor is required. Sequential decomposition computes residual against the F32 sum of all previously restored terms, casts the amplified residual with E4 RNE, and restores with the reciprocal amplification. Merely scaling the latest un-restored FP8 residual is not this algorithm.

Installed SDK0.8.1 `src/float/f8e4.rs` and `src/scalar.rs` were read. E4 max finite448, minimum subnormal2^-9. Independent sorted-lattice RNE agrees with a literal translation of SDK conversion at all77,312 term-cast points per scheme. Both signs checked. Exhaustive domain is19,328 nonnegative F32 values with <=8 significant bits through minF32 subnormal, <=255, a conservative normalized BF16-significand domain. This models scalar arithmetic, not target cast FTZ or native accumulation.

| amplification | max cast inputs | max normalized absolute reconstruction error | sufficient exact domain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| requested `[1,16,256,4096]` | `[255,128,2^-6,2^-6]` | `2^-22` | `|u| >= 2^-14` |
| residual-bound-derived `[1,16,2^22,2^27]` | `[255,128,256,256]` | `2^-37` | `|u| >= 2^-29` |

For the derived variant, the residual after hi+lo16 is at most2^-14; third amplification2^22 is the largest power of two that stays within448. After this third term the actual maximum residual is2^-19, so fourth amplification2^27 is safe. Earlier informal2^40 speculation is WRONG: u=0.0000324249267578125 gives fourth cast input2,097,152 and saturates to448. This is a numerical range derivation, not a performance parameter search or an implementation selection.

Both schemes reconstruct E47 case7 `[+1,-1,254*2^-22]` and the prior independent-residual counterexample `[+.53125,-.53125,254*2^-23]` exactly. Actual current `generate_references.scaled_projection` plus `gemma4.RMSNorm.forward` were invoked with only a1×4096 weight row then expanded projected output (no full3840×4096 matrix). Expected/actual final values are respectively0.609375 and0.59765625, all3840 outputs exact for these two CPU fixtures. Original O tolerance is atol.05, rtol.01, not the FFN .01 absolute tolerance.

The current public O input generator is `BF16(-1+2*k/2^24)`. Its nonnegative scalar support consists of2,177 values: below2^-16, the multiples of2^-23; above that, the BF16 grid through1. Checking21,887 admissible scalar/amax-scale pairs across24 amax binades found no reconstruction mismatch with either four-term scheme. This does not prove a joint PRNG fixture, QKV's post-RMS activation distribution, reduction equivalence, or NPU correctness. In particular post-RMS QKV has no inherited O-grid guarantee.

A BF16 tiny2^-29 with amax1 lies numerically within[-1,1] but outside that generator grid. Requested fixed4 loses it; derived factors preserve it. In a cancellation fixture the current PyTorch full-K F32 dot is0.00159454345703125 whereas decomposed term sums give0.0018167495727539062, even with exact reconstructed activations. Both happen to round to final0.5 after RMS/residual here. This directly illustrates why scalar exactness must not be relabelled dot/whole-kernel exactness.

Restoring `[s,s/16,...]` before term reduction is not globally equivalent to summing normalized terms then multiplying by s: normalized255 at s=2^120 has finite common-scale result3.3895313892515355e38, but hi256*s overflows F32 before negative residual cancellation. This example is outside public O activation range. More generally coefficients may underflow at extreme scales; F32 x/s may already lose extreme dynamic-range inputs. Smaller normalized values remain inexact under both schemes. No arbitrary-BF16 or full-model claim.

## Three architecture principles and their exact SDK consequences

1. **Spend conversion work on the smaller reused activation, retain supplied weight dtype.** MSD supports this principle, not an FP8 accuracy theorem: its FP8 error bound is explicitly TBD and its MXFP4 E1M2 definition differs from supplied Gemma E2M1. SDK `ContractionWeight` supports E4×E4, E5×E5, BF16×BF16 only; E4×E5 and E4×BF16 are absent. Use activation decomposition plus F32 coefficient restoration, not mixed-dtype claims. Outer TimeBroadcast reuses a fetched weight packet without refetching; additional native contraction time is real, but 4terms do not automatically imply4HBM/DM weight reads. E47/E53 numerical history requires these new precision boundaries.

2. **Choose the register-resident operand to expose useful output parallelism.** Native E4 weight→plain Sub StoTRF avoids C10's unsupported Main lookup→BF16 StoTRF edge. Q8rows×3840=30,720B/slice and KV4rows×3840=15,360B fit the frontend64KiB TRF payload bound; put output rows in Lane8/4 and stream activation terms. This is different from old activation-resident TRF/Term-lane paths. Costs: complete FP8 weight Sub staging, activation term traffic, TRF lifetime/read contention, F32 output handoff when channel scale must follow term sum. O requires careful extra row axis: the simple15row layout cannot be assumed to fit the VE8accumulator contract and may require repeated activation stream. Neither bank bandwidth nor whole speedup follows from Lane count. E132's losing K4 mapping is not made winning merely by calling it output-owned.

3. **Keep decode, contraction, scale and local reduction on a supported producer→consumer path; independent projections need not be concatenated in memory.** RNGD Main FP4 lookup→E5 native→VE is already supported in E134. Applying it independently to compact up and gate storage can remove both full decoded weight materialization and E131's packed joint assembly, while preserving blockscale/row consumer coordinates. It adds repeated lookup/setup and consumes different Main/Sub/register lifetimes. E135 is the parent's concrete implementation. `begin_interleaved` and `cluster_tile` public names alone are insufficient: prior target failures remain. Runtime repacking is real work, and papers' offline packing is unavailable under unchanged MOA input contracts.

SDK detail: `vector_intra_slice_reduce(Add)` uses `ReduceAccTree`; `IntraFirst` then widen→`vector_inter_slice_reduce(Add)` is explicitly supported and already used by current E134 down reduction. It is not the old E47 pair `AddF` zip path. Thus a term reducer followed by O column reducer does not intrinsically require a large F32 spill; exact new shape/lowering still needs validation. Conversely IntraSliceReduce→ordinary Fp Mul is not a public stage transition, so QKV channel scale after term sum needs a separate pass or an explicitly validated arithmetic redesign.

## Primary sources opened in this audit

- Furiosa official Outer, current page read2026-09-27: https://developer.furiosa.ai/furiosa-opt/book/computing-tensors/contraction-engine/outer.html — Broadcast/Performance/Operand types. TimeBroadcast amortizes fetch; Lane1/2/4/8; storage packet32/64B. No application speedup inference.
- Furiosa official register files: https://developer.furiosa.ai/furiosa-opt/book/computing-tensors/register-files.html — lane storage, DM-direct StoTRF, double buffering and shared-bank contention. Hardware80KB is not frontend64KiB logical capacity.
- Furiosa official kernel design: https://developer.furiosa.ai/furiosa-opt/book/quick-start/kernel-design.html — GEMV maps output rows spatially and reuses the full activation; a teaching example, not proof the current O ingress can be replaced cheaply.
- Furiosa TCP paper, ISCA2024, author-hosted primary PDF: https://web.ist.utl.pt/nuno.lopes/pubs/tcp-isca24.pdf — flexible contraction primitive; fixed GPU/TPU matmul padding arguments do not transfer without mapping analysis.
- Multi-Scale Dequant, arXiv2605.13915v1 (first submission2026-05-13; HTML body displays2026-08-24): https://arxiv.org/html/2605.13915v1 — §§3.1,3.8,3.9: activation-side decomposition, shared resident weights, streaming partial reconstruction. Numerical simulations/latency models, not measured RNGD acceleration; FP8 boundTBD.
- FP8 Formats for Deep Learning,2022-09-12: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.05433 — E4M3 finite448/minsubnormal2^-9; installedSDK source independently confirms the actual format used. Format paper is not our decomposition accuracy proof.
- W4A16 Mixed-Precision Matrix Multiplication on Decoupled Architecture,2026-01-23: https://arxiv.org/html/2601.16536v1 — §§3,4.2: global-workspace handoff between Ascend vector and cube limits gains. Our DM is on-chip; do not rename it HBM or inherit Ascend speedups.
- Mapping Gemma3 onto an Edge Dataflow Architecture, arXiv2602.06063v1: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.06063v1 — §3.2.1 FusedDQP. Decode and projection share the consuming compute tile; Q4NX format/offline rearrangement/AMD DMA do not directly map to fixed Gemma FP4 input.
- LiquidGEMM,2025-09-01: https://arxiv.org/html/2509.01229v1 — §§4,5.1–5.4: dequant instructions, pipeline and operand orientation must be chosen together. Requires offline LiquidQuant transform/PTX/TMA; not an executable MOA technique.
- FASQ, arXiv2605.04084v1: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.04084v1 — §4.1 and Table1: its batch1 direct-compute kernel outperforms its LUT route. PQ codebooks/requantized weights differ from current weights. Its per-thread table-usage argument must not be generalized to aggregate LUT reuse or to RNGD's fixed decode tables.
- SPECTRA,2026-09-21: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.24847v1 — §3.1.1 switches an8×8 FPGA array into8dot lanes for GEMV. Newly reconfigured hardware; RNGD already has TCP lanes, so no claim of recovering a conventional systolic padding loss.
- LUT-GEMM, ICLR2024 / arXiv2206.09557v4: https://arxiv.org/html/2206.09557v4 and T-MAC author repository https://github.com/microsoft/T-MAC/ — activation-dependent LUT and bitplane representations need table construction/index operations. RNGD `fetch_table_lookup<OutD>` is a dtype-selected fixed conversion with no public runtime table handle. SAIL https://arxiv.org/html/2509.25853v1 additionally requires new C-SRAM hardware/ISA. These are not immediately feasible replacement kernels on installedSDK0.8.1.
